1. What is collected

When you create an account, Whetstone stores your email address and a hashed form of your password.

When you use Whetstone, it stores:

  • the entries you write, with their date and time;
  • the judgements, gains, levels, skills and classes attached to your account;
  • the time zone your days are counted in.
